What is a Dental Implant?

A Dental Implant is a small, screw-like post made of biocompatible titanium that acts as an artificial tooth root. It is surgically inserted into the jawbone, where it provides a stable foundation for a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture. Unlike other tooth-replacement options, implants are a standalone solution that does not rely on neighboring teeth for support.

The Science of Osseointegration

The secret to the success of dental implants is a biological process called Osseointegration. Titanium has the unique ability to fuse directly with living bone. Over several months, your jawbone grows around the implant post, locking it into place. This not only provides unmatched stability but also prevents jawbone resorption (bone loss) that naturally occurs when a tooth is missing.

Why Choose Implants Over Dentures?

Dental implants offer several lifestyle and health advantages over traditional removable options:

  • Unrestricted Diet: Eat hard, crunchy, or chewy foods without fear of your teeth slipping.
  • Speech Clarity: Unlike bulky dentures, implants do not interfere with your tongue or speech.
  • Durability: While bridges may last 10 years, an implant can last a lifetime with proper care.
  • Facial Esthetics: By preserving the jawbone, implants prevent the "collapsed" facial appearance associated with tooth loss.

The 3-Step Implant Process

The journey to a new smile typically takes place over 3 to 6 months to allow for proper healing:

  1. Placement: The titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation.
  2. Healing (Abutment): Once osseointegration is complete, a small connector called an abutment is attached to the post.
  3. Restoration: A custom-crafted porcelain crown is placed on top, perfectly matched to the shade and shape of your natural teeth.

The Role of Bone Grafting

For an implant to be successful, there must be sufficient bone density to support it. If the jawbone has thinned due to long-term tooth loss, we perform a Bone Graft. This procedure uses specialized bone-growth material to "bulk up" the area, ensuring a solid foundation for your future implant.

All-on-4® and Full-Arch Restoration

For patients missing all or most of their teeth, we offer All-on-4® technology. This revolutionary technique uses just four strategically placed implants to support an entire arch of permanent teeth. It is often referred to as "Teeth in a Day" because patients can leave with a functional, beautiful smile much faster than with traditional methods.

Who is an Ideal Candidate?

Most adults with missing teeth are candidates for implants. You are an ideal candidate if:

  • You have healthy gums and are free of periodontal disease.
  • You have adequate bone density (or are willing to undergo a graft).
  • You are a non-smoker or willing to quit during the healing phase.
  • You are committed to good oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups.
