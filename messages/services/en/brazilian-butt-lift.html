What is a Brazilian Butt Lift?

A Brazilian Butt Lift (BBL) is a specialized fat transfer procedure that augments the size and reshapes the contour of the buttocks without the use of synthetic implants. By using your own fat, the procedure provides a more natural look and feel while simultaneously slimming down "trouble areas" like the abdomen, flanks, or thighs.

The Dual Benefit: Contouring & Enhancement

The BBL is often called a "2-in-1" procedure because it addresses two aesthetic goals at once:

  • Strategic Liposuction: We remove unwanted fat from areas like the "love handles" or stomach to create a narrower waistline (the "snatched" look).
  • Natural Volumization: The harvested fat is purified and re-injected into the buttocks to add projection, lift, and fullness.

BBL vs. Butt Implants

While both procedures add volume, the BBL is widely preferred for several reasons:

  • No Foreign Material: Uses your own biological tissue, eliminating the risk of implant rejection or capsular contracture.
  • Customizable Shape: Surgeons can "art-sculpt" the fat to fill in hip dips or focus specifically on upper-pole projection.
  • Softer Feel: The results feel identical to natural body tissue because they are natural body tissue.

The Procedure Step-by-Step

A BBL typically takes between 2 to 4 hours and is performed under general anesthesia:

  1. Fat Harvesting: Using gentle liposuction, fat is collected from the donor sites (waist, back, or thighs).
  2. Purification: The collected fat is processed in a centrifuge to separate healthy fat cells from impurities and fluids.
  3. Micro-Infiltration: The purified fat is injected into the gluteal region at various depths to ensure maximum blood supply and graft survival.
  4. Closing: Small liposuction incisions are closed with dissolvable stitches.

Recovery & Post-Op Guidelines

The recovery phase is the most critical part of ensuring "fat take" (the survival of the injected cells):

  • The Golden Rule: You must avoid sitting directly on your buttocks for at least 2 to 3 weeks. Specialized BBL pillows are used when sitting is necessary.
  • Compression: You will wear a professional-grade faja (compression garment) to reduce swelling and mold the liposuctioned areas.
  • Lymphatic Massage: We recommend specialized massages starting in week 1 to prevent fluid buildup and ensure smooth results.

Safety First: Our Protocol

Safety is our absolute priority. We follow the latest international surgical guidelines, which include:

  • Subcutaneous Injection: We only inject fat into the space just under the skin, never into the deep muscle, to eliminate the risk of fat embolism.
  • VTE Prevention: Strict protocols to prevent blood clots, including early ambulation (walking) post-surgery.

Ideal Candidates

You may be a great candidate for a BBL if you:

  • Have enough "donor fat" in other areas of the body to harvest.
  • Are a non-smoker, as nicotine significantly reduces the survival rate of the transferred fat.
  • Can commit to the strict recovery period (not sitting/laying on your back).
  • Seek a more "hourglass" figure with improved gluteal projection.
