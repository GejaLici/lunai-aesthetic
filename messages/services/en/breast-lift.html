What is a Breast Lift?

A Breast Lift, or Mastopexy, is a surgical procedure designed to raise and firm the breasts by removing excess skin and tightening the surrounding tissue. Over time, factors such as pregnancy, breastfeeding, weight fluctuations, and gravity can cause the skin to lose elasticity. A lift restores a perkier profile by shifting the breast mass higher on the chest wall.

Breast Lift vs. Augmentation

It is important to understand the difference between these two popular procedures:

  • Breast Lift: Primarily addresses position and sagging. It removes loose skin but does not significantly change the size of the breast.
  • Breast Augmentation: Specifically adds volume using implants or fat transfer.
  • Augmentation-Mastopexy: Many patients choose to combine both procedures to achieve both a lift and increased fullness simultaneously.

Incision Techniques: Choosing the Right Method

The choice of incision depends on the degree of sagging (ptosis) and the amount of skin to be removed:

  • Crescent Lift: A small incision along the top half of the areola. Ideal for very mild sagging.
  • Peri-Areolar (Donut) Lift: A circular incision around the areola. Best for moderate sagging and areola reduction.
  • Vertical (Lollipop) Lift: Incisions around the areola and vertically down to the breast crease. This is the gold standard for most patients.
  • Inverted-T (Anchor) Lift: Adds a horizontal incision along the breast crease. Used for significant sagging or breast reduction cases.

The Surgical Process

Mastopexy is performed under general anesthesia and typically takes **2 to 3 hours**:

  1. Skin Removal: The surgeon removes the pre-determined area of excess skin.
  2. Reshaping: The underlying breast tissue is lifted and secured with internal sutures.
  3. Nipple Repositioning: The nipple and areola are moved to a higher, more youthful position (remaining attached to blood supply).
  4. Areola Reduction: If the areolae have stretched, they can be resized during this step for better proportion.

Recovery and Aftercare

Following post-operative instructions is vital for maintaining the "lift":

  • Support: You must wear a specialized surgical support bra for 4–6 weeks to support the new internal structure.
  • Activity: Most patients return to desk work in 1 week. Heavy lifting and high-impact exercise (like running) must be avoided for at least a month.
  • Sensation: Temporary changes in nipple sensation are common and usually resolve within a few months.

Scar Management and Healing

While a breast lift involves more incisions than an augmentation, modern techniques ensure scars are thin and well-placed:

  • Placement: Incisions are hidden along the natural borders of the areola and the breast fold.
  • Evolution: Scars will appear firm and pink for the first few months but will soften and fade into thin white lines by the 12-month mark.
  • Care: We recommend silicone gel or sheets starting 3-4 weeks post-op to optimize scar healing.

Are You a Good Candidate?

You may benefit from a Mastopexy if:

  • Your nipples point downward or fall below the breast crease.
  • Your breasts have a "flat" or elongated shape rather than a round contour.
  • You have reached a stable weight (further weight loss can cause re-sagging).
  • You have realistic expectations about the surgical scars required to achieve a lift.
