What is Lipolysis?

Lipolysis is a minimally invasive or non-surgical body contouring procedure that breaks down and "dissolves" localized fat deposits. Unlike weight loss, which shrinks fat cells, lipolysis removes or destroys them entirely. This procedure is specifically designed for stubborn areas that are resistant to diet and exercise, such as the double chin, love handles, or inner thighs.

Types of Lipolysis: Laser, VASER, and Chemical

Depending on your goals and the amount of fat being treated, we offer several advanced modalities:

  • Laser Lipolysis (SmartLipo): Uses laser energy to melt fat before it is removed or absorbed. The heat also stimulates collagen production, providing a skin-tightening effect.
  • VASER Lipolysis: Utilizes ultrasound frequency to gently vibrate and loosen fat cells, making them easier to remove while preserving surrounding connective tissues.
  • Injection Lipolysis (Fat Dissolving): A non-surgical method where deoxycholic acid or similar compounds are injected into the fat layer to chemically dissolve cell membranes.

Popular Treatment Areas

Lipolysis is ideal for "sculpting" smaller, specific areas where precision is required:

  • Submental: Eliminating a "double chin" and defining the jawline.
  • Abdomen & Flanks: Sculpting the waistline and removing "love handles."
  • Arms: Reducing the "bat wing" area for a more toned look.
  • Thighs & Knees: Contouring the inner/outer thighs and smoothing the knee area.

The Procedure Step-by-Step

While the process varies by modality, a typical Lipolysis session follows these steps:

  1. Mapping: The treatment area is precisely marked while the patient is in an upright position.
  2. Anesthesia: Local anesthesia or tumescent fluid is applied to the area to ensure a painless experience.
  3. Fat Breakdown: The laser or ultrasound probe is inserted (or injections are administered) to liquefy the fat cells.
  4. Removal (if applicable): In surgical lipolysis, a micro-cannula is used to gently suction out the liquefied fat.

Recovery and Results Timeline

Lipolysis offers a much faster recovery compared to traditional surgery:

  • Downtime: Most patients return to work within 1 to 2 days for non-surgical methods and 3 to 5 days for laser/VASER methods.
  • Compression: A light compression garment may be required for a few weeks to minimize swelling and support the skin's new shape.
  • Visible Results: You will notice an immediate change, but the final contour becomes visible at 3 to 6 months as the body processes the fat and the skin tightens.

Lipolysis vs. Traditional Liposuction

It is important to understand which procedure best fits your needs:

Feature Lipolysis Traditional Liposuction
Fat Volume Small, localized pockets Large volumes of fat
Skin Tightening High (Laser/VASER versions) Minimal
Downtime Short (2-4 days) Longer (1-2 weeks)

Who is an Ideal Candidate?

Lipolysis is a contouring tool, not a weight-loss solution. You are an ideal candidate if:

  • You are within 10–15% of your ideal body weight.
  • You have localized fat deposits that do not respond to diet.
  • You have good skin elasticity (or are seeking the skin-tightening benefits of Laser Lipolysis).
  • You have realistic expectations and a healthy lifestyle.
