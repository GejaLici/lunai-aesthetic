What is Lip Reshaping?

Lip Reshaping encompasses a variety of surgical procedures designed to permanently alter the size, shape, or position of the lips. While dermal fillers are excellent for temporary volume, surgery addresses structural issues—such as an elongated space between the nose and lip or excessive lip thickness—that injections cannot fix. The result is a more balanced, youthful, and symmetrical facial profile.

The Bullhorn Lip Lift: Youthful Elevation

As we age, the upper lip tends to lengthen and sag, covering the upper teeth. The Subnasal Lip Lift (or Bullhorn Lift) is the most popular reshaping technique:

  • shortens the Philtrum: Reduces the distance between the base of the nose and the top of the lip.
  • Increases "Tooth Show": Reveals more of the upper teeth for a more youthful "smile window."
  • Enhances Vermilion Border: Naturally rolls the pink part of the lip outward, creating volume without a "duck-like" appearance.

Lip Reduction: Refining Volume

For individuals with naturally oversized lips that feel out of proportion or cause functional discomfort, Lip Reduction offers a solution. The surgeon removes a thin strip of tissue from the "wet-dry" line (the inside of the lip), effectively narrowing the lip profile while keeping scars completely hidden inside the mouth.

The Procedure Step-by-Step

Lip reshaping is typically an outpatient procedure performed under local anesthesia or light sedation, taking 45 to 60 minutes:

  1. Precision Marking: For a lift, the surgeon marks a small "bullhorn" shaped area of skin directly beneath the nostrils.
  2. Tissue Refinement: The excess skin (for a lift) or mucosal tissue (for a reduction) is carefully removed.
  3. Muscular Support: In a lip lift, the underlying tissue is often anchored to ensure the result remains stable over time.
  4. Closure: Very fine sutures are used to close the incision, ensuring the thinnest possible scar.

Recovery and Scar Management

The lips are highly vascular and heal quickly, though initial swelling is significant:

  • Days 1-3: Peak swelling. Cold compresses are essential. You may be advised to stick to a soft-food diet.
  • Day 7: Sutures are removed. Most patients can return to work at this point, as redness can be covered with concealer.
  • The Scar: For a lip lift, the scar is hidden in the natural shadow beneath the nose. Within 3-6 months, it typically becomes nearly invisible.

Surgery vs. Lip Fillers

Understanding which option is right for you is crucial for satisfaction:

  • Fillers: Best for adding volume and hydration. They are temporary and require maintenance every 6-12 months.
  • Surgery: Best for positioning and proportions. Surgery is a permanent one-time fix that doesn't "migrate" like fillers sometimes can.

Ideal Candidates

You may be a candidate for lip reshaping if:

  • You have a long philtrum (more than 15mm) that makes your face look aged.
  • Your upper teeth are not visible when your mouth is slightly open.
  • You have had "filler fatigue" and want a permanent solution.
  • You feel your lips are disproportionately large for your other facial features.
