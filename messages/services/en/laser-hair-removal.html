What is Laser Hair Removal?

Laser Hair Removal is a medical procedure that uses a concentrated beam of light (laser) to remove unwanted hair. During the process, the laser emits a light that is absorbed by the pigment (melanin) in the hair. The light energy is converted to heat, which damages the hair follicles that produce hairs. This damage inhibits or delays future hair growth, leading to permanent hair reduction.

The Science: Selective Photothermolysis

The success of the treatment relies on Selective Photothermolysis. This means the laser targets the dark pigment of the hair without damaging the surrounding skin. By delivering energy specifically to the hair bulb, the laser effectively "shuts down" the follicle's ability to regenerate a new hair shaft.

Popular Treatment Areas

Laser hair removal is versatile and can be used on almost any part of the body where unwanted hair grows:

  • Face: Upper lip, chin, and sideburns.
  • Body: Underarms, back, chest, and shoulders.
  • Legs & Arms: Full or half legs and forearms.
  • Bikini Area: Standard bikini line or full Brazilian.

What to Expect During Your Session

A typical session takes anywhere from 10 to 60 minutes, depending on the area size:

  1. Preparation: The area is cleaned, and the hair is shaved (if not done previously) to just a few millimeters above the skin.
  2. Protection: Both the patient and the provider wear specialized eye protection.
  3. Treatment: A handheld laser device is pressed against the skin. Most modern lasers feature a cooling tip to soothe the skin and minimize discomfort.
  4. Sensation: Most patients describe the feeling as a "warm pinch" or a "rubber band snap" against the skin.

Why Multiple Sessions are Necessary

Hair grows in different cycles: Anagen (growth), Catagen (transition), and Telogen (resting). The laser is only effective on hair that is currently in the Anagen phase. Because not all hairs are growing at the same time, a series of 6 to 8 sessions (spaced 4–8 weeks apart) is required to ensure every follicle is targeted during its active phase.

Laser Technology for All Skin Tones

Advancements in laser technology mean that treatment is no longer limited to those with light skin and dark hair:

  • Alexandrite Lasers: Ideal for lighter skin tones with dark hair.
  • Nd:YAG Lasers: The gold standard for darker or tanned skin, as it bypasses the melanin in the skin to safely target the hair root.
  • Diode Lasers: Highly effective for a wide range of skin and hair types.

Are You an Ideal Candidate?

You may be a candidate for laser hair removal if:

  • You have unwanted hair that causes self-consciousness or irritation.
  • You suffer from frequent ingrown hairs (folliculitis).
  • You want a long-term solution to avoid the hassle of temporary hair removal methods.
  • Note: Laser is least effective on white, gray, red, or very light blond hair, as these lack the pigment necessary to absorb the laser's energy.
