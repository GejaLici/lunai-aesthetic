What is Multivitamin IV Treatment?

Multivitamin IV Therapy is a wellness treatment that delivers a high-concentration blend of vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants directly into the bloodstream. By bypassing the digestive system, this "vitamin drip" ensures that your cells receive the full benefit of the nutrients instantly, providing a level of replenishment that is impossible to achieve through diet or oral supplements alone.

Oral vs. IV: The Absorption Gap

The primary advantage of intravenous treatment is Bioavailability. When you swallow a vitamin pill, the body only absorbs roughly 20–30% of the nutrients due to the "first-pass" metabolism in the liver and gut. With IV therapy, absorption is 100%, allowing for therapeutic doses that can rapidly reverse deficiencies and boost cellular function.

What to Expect During Your Infusion

An IV multivitamin session is a relaxing experience that takes about 30 to 45 minutes:

  1. Consultation: A medical professional reviews your health history and goals.
  2. Placement: A small, thin catheter is gently inserted into a vein (usually in the arm).
  3. Infusion: You can relax, read, or use your phone while the nutrient-rich saline drip enters your system.
  4. Immediate Effect: Many patients report feeling a "metallic taste" or a warm sensation as the vitamins circulate, followed by a surge in mental clarity.
