What is a Hair Transplant?

A Hair Transplant is a minimally invasive surgical procedure that redistributes healthy hair follicles from a "donor area" (typically the back and sides of the head) to thinning or bald areas (the "recipient area"). Because the donor follicles are genetically resistant to the hormones that cause balding, the transplanted hair continues to grow naturally for a lifetime.

Modern Techniques: FUE vs. DHI

We specialize in the two most advanced methods of hair restoration, both of which avoid the traditional "strip" method and leave no linear scars:

  • FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction): Individual follicles are extracted and then placed into tiny channels created in the recipient area. This is ideal for covering large areas of baldness.
  • DHI (Direct Hair Implantation): Using a specialized Choi Implanter Pen, follicles are extracted and implanted simultaneously. This technique allows for maximum density and precise control over the angle and direction of hair growth.
  • Sapphire FUE: A variation of FUE using blades made from precious sapphire, which creates smoother micro-channels for faster healing and higher graft survival.

The Procedure Step-by-Step

A hair transplant is performed under local anesthesia and typically takes **6 to 8 hours**:

  1. Consultation & Hairline Design: The surgeon marks the new hairline, ensuring it complements your facial proportions and age.
  2. Extraction: Individual follicular units are harvested using a motorized micro-punch tool.
  3. Graft Preparation: Follicles are cleaned and sorted in a specialized solution to maintain their viability.
  4. Implantation: The grafts are meticulously placed into the recipient sites, following the natural flow of your existing hair.

Recovery and Post-Op Care

The recovery phase is quick, and most patients return to work within a few days:

  • Days 1-3: Minimal swelling and redness. You will follow a specialized washing protocol.
  • Day 10: The small scabs in the donor and recipient areas typically fall off, leaving a clean appearance.
  • Activity: Avoid heavy exercise and direct sunlight for at least 2 to 4 weeks to protect the new grafts.

The Growth Timeline: What to Expect

Patience is essential, as hair growth follows a biological cycle:

  • 2-4 Weeks: "Shock Loss" occurs where the transplanted hair shafts fall out. This is normal; the follicles remain healthy.
  • 3-5 Months: New hair begins to emerge from the scalp. It may initially be thin or curly.
  • 6-9 Months: Significant growth and thickening occur. Most patients see 60-70% of the final result.
  • 12-18 Months: The final result is achieved with full density and natural texture.

Are the Results Permanent?

Yes. Because the donor hair is taken from the "safe zone" at the back of the head—hair that is not affected by DHT (dihydrotestosterone)—the transplanted follicles will continue to grow for the rest of your life. While your non-transplanted hair may continue to thin over time, the transplanted area remains stable.

Ideal Candidates for Restoration

You may be a candidate for a hair transplant if you:

  • Have a stable donor area with sufficient hair density.
  • Experience Male or Female Pattern Baldness (Androgenetic Alopecia).
  • Have realistic expectations about what can be achieved with the available donor hair.
  • Are in good health and have no scalp conditions that might impede healing.
