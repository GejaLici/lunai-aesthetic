What is Microdermabrasion?

Microdermabrasion is a minimally invasive epidermal resurfacing procedure used to renew overall skin tone and texture. By gently "sanding" the skin with a specialized applicator, the treatment removes the thick, uneven outer layer (the stratum corneum). This process triggers the body’s natural healing response, promoting the growth of new, healthy skin cells and stimulating collagen production.

The Science: Physical Exfoliation

Unlike chemical peels that use acids to dissolve skin bonds, microdermabrasion uses mechanical force. The combination of gentle abrasion and vacuum suction provides a dual benefit:

  • Exfoliation: Removes the buildup of dead cells, debris, and pollutants.
  • Circulation: The vacuum action increases blood flow to the surface, aiding in lymphatic drainage and cellular oxygenation.
  • Product Absorption: Post-treatment, your skincare products can penetrate up to 50% deeper into the skin.

Crystal vs. Diamond-Tip Systems

We utilize the two most effective methods for mechanical resurfacing:

  • Diamond-Tip Microdermabrasion: Uses a wand with a diamond-encrusted tip to gently abrade the skin. This is the preferred method for sensitive areas near the eyes and mouth, as there is no risk of particles entering the eye.
  • Crystal Microdermabrasion: Uses a wand that sprays a high-speed stream of fine crystals (usually aluminum oxide or sodium bicarbonate) onto the skin, which are then vacuumed away.

Skin Concerns Treated

Microdermabrasion is an excellent "maintenance" treatment for a variety of common skin issues:

  • Fine Lines: Softens early signs of aging.
  • Sun Damage: Lightens mild hyperpigmentation and age spots.
  • Acne Scarring: Smooths out superficial pitting and post-inflammatory marks.
  • Enlarged Pores: Deep cleans pores to make them appear smaller.
  • Dullness: Restores a healthy, "lit-from-within" glow to tired skin.

What to Expect During Your Session

A microdermabrasion treatment is often called a "lunchtime facial" because it is fast and painless:

  1. Cleansing: The skin is thoroughly cleansed to remove oils and makeup.
  2. Resurfacing: The provider moves the wand across the face in a systematic pattern. You will feel a mild "scrubbing" sensation and gentle suction.
  3. Hydration: A soothing mask or antioxidant-rich serum is applied to the fresh skin.
  4. Protection: A broad-spectrum SPF is applied to protect the newly exposed skin.

Aftercare and Results

There is zero downtime required. You may notice slight redness for an hour or two, similar to a mild windburn. To maintain your results:

  • Moisturize: Keep the skin hydrated as it may feel slightly tight for 24 hours.
  • Sun Protection: Your skin will be more sensitive to UV rays; SPF 30+ is mandatory.
  • Frequency: For best results, we recommend a series of 5 to 10 treatments spaced 2 to 4 weeks apart.

Ideal Candidates

Microdermabrasion is safe for almost all skin types and tones. You are an ideal candidate if:

  • You want a non-chemical way to refresh your complexion.
  • You have minor skin imperfections you wish to smooth over.
  • You are looking for a treatment with no recovery time.
  • Note: This treatment is not recommended for those with active cystic acne, rosacea, or open sores.
