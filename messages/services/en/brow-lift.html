What is a Brow Lift?

A Brow Lift, also known as a Forehead Lift, is a surgical procedure designed to raise the eyebrows and reduce the appearance of wrinkles on the forehead and the bridge of the nose. As we age, the brow can descend, creating a "hooded" look over the eyes and making one appear tired, sad, or angry. This procedure restores the brow to its natural, youthful position.

Brow Lift vs. Eyelid Surgery (Blepharoplasty)

It is common to confuse these two procedures, as both address the eye area:

  • Brow Lift: Specifically targets the position of the eyebrow. If your upper eyelid sagging is caused by the brow dropping, a lift is the correct solution.
  • Blepharoplasty: Targets excess skin on the eyelid itself. Many patients achieve the best results by combining these two surgeries to fully rejuvenate the upper third of the face.

Modern Surgical Techniques

We utilize minimally invasive methods to ensure faster recovery and discreet results:

  • Endoscopic Brow Lift: The gold standard. Using a tiny camera (endoscope) and small incisions hidden behind the hairline, the tissue is lifted and secured. Benefits: Minimal scarring and less numbness.
  • Temporal (Lateral) Lift: Focuses on lifting the outer corners of the brow. This is ideal for correcting "hooding" at the edges of the eyes.
  • Coronal Lift: A traditional technique involving an incision from ear to ear across the top of the head. Typically reserved for patients with significant skin laxity.

The Procedure Step-by-Step

The surgery is typically performed under general anesthesia or intravenous sedation and takes 1 to 2 hours:

  1. Incision: Small, strategically placed incisions are made within the hair-bearing scalp to hide future scars.
  2. Tissue Release: The surgeon carefully releases the attachments that hold the brow in a lowered position.
  3. Elevation & Securing: The forehead skin is lifted, and the eyebrows are repositioned. We use specialized internal anchors to hold the brow in place while it heals.
  4. Closure: Incisions are closed with sutures or specialized clips.

Recovery and Aftercare

Initial healing is quick, with most patients returning to their routine within 10 days:

  • First 48 Hours: Swelling and bruising around the eyes are common. Keeping the head elevated is essential.
  • Week 1: Sutures or clips are removed. Any residual bruising can usually be camouflaged with makeup.
  • Week 2-3: Most patients feel comfortable appearing in public and returning to non-strenuous work.

Expected Results & Longevity

A brow lift provides long-lasting rejuvenation:

  • Immediate Effect: You will notice an "opened" appearance to the eyes and a smoother forehead immediately.
  • Longevity: While the aging process continues, the results of a brow lift typically last 10 to 15 years.
  • Natural Look: Modern techniques avoid the "surprised" look by focusing on subtle, vertical elevation.

Ideal Candidates

You may be a candidate for a brow lift if you experience:

  • Deep horizontal furrows across the forehead.
  • Frown lines between the eyebrows (the "11s").
  • Sagging or low-positioned brows that create a heavy look over the eyes.
  • Difficulty applying eye makeup due to skin "crowding" the upper lid.
