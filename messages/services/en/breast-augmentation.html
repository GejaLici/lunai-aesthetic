What is Breast Augmentation?

Breast Augmentation, often referred to as a "boob job," is a surgical procedure designed to increase breast size, improve shape, and enhance overall body symmetry. Whether you are looking to restore volume lost after pregnancy/weight loss or desire a more balanced profile, modern augmentation focuses on creating a look that complements your unique frame.

Choosing Your Implants: Silicone vs. Saline

We offer a variety of FDA-approved implants to meet your aesthetic goals:

  • Silicone Gel Implants: Known for their natural feel and "memory" gel that mimics real breast tissue.
  • Saline Implants: Filled with sterile salt water; they often require smaller incisions and can be adjusted for volume during surgery.
  • Gummy Bear Implants: Highly cohesive silicone that maintains a stable shape (teardrop) even if the shell is compromised.
  • Round vs. Teardrop: Choose round for upper-pole fullness or teardrop (anatomical) for a more gradual, natural slope.

Placement: Submuscular vs. Subglandular

The positioning of the implant plays a major role in the final look and long-term stability:

  • Submuscular (Under the Muscle): The implant is placed behind the pectoral muscle. This offers more coverage, reduces the visibility of "rippling," and may lower the risk of capsular contracture.
  • Subglandular (Over the Muscle): The implant is placed between the breast tissue and the muscle. This typically involves a shorter recovery time and less discomfort during the initial healing phase.

The Procedure Step-by-Step

Breast augmentation is typically an outpatient procedure performed under general anesthesia:

  1. Incision Placement: Options include the inframammary fold (under the breast), periareolar (around the nipple), or transaxillary (armpit) to ensure scars are as discreet as possible.
  2. Pocket Creation: The surgeon carefully creates a space either above or below the pectoral muscle.
  3. Implant Insertion: Using sterile techniques (often with a Keller Funnel), the implant is precisely positioned.
  4. Closing: The incisions are closed with multi-layer sutures and dressed with surgical tape or bandages.

Recovery and Healing Timeline

Understanding the "drop and fluff" process is key to managing expectations:

  • Days 1-5: Some tightness and soreness in the chest area. A specialized support bra must be worn 24/7.
  • Week 2: Most patients return to light work and daily activities. Exercise is still restricted.
  • Month 1-2: Swelling subsides, and the implants begin to "settle" into a more natural position.
  • 6 Months: The final shape is achieved, and surgical scars begin to fade significantly.

Safety: Capsular Contracture & Monitoring

Modern breast surgery is safer than ever, but we ensure our patients are educated on:

  • Capsular Contracture: The body’s natural reaction to a foreign object; we use techniques to minimize scar tissue hardening.
  • Implant Longevity: While implants are not lifetime devices, many last 10-20 years. We recommend regular ultrasound or MRI screenings for silicone patients.

Ideal Candidates for Augmentation

Breast augmentation is suitable for women who:

  • Are at least 18 (for saline) or 22 (for silicone) years of age.
  • Are in good overall health and do not have active breast cancer.
  • Are not currently pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Seek to correct asymmetry or loss of volume due to life changes.
