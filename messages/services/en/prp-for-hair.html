What is PRP for Hair Loss?

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy for hair is a regenerative medical treatment where a patient’s own blood is processed to create a concentrated solution of platelets. These platelets are rich in growth factors that, when injected into the scalp, act as a "fertilizer" for hair follicles. It is a safe, proactive biological tool used to stop hair shedding and increase the diameter of existing hair shafts.

The Science: Awakening Dormant Follicles

Hair follicles that are thinning are often "miniaturized"—they produce thinner, shorter, and more brittle hairs over time. PRP reverses this process by:

  • Extending the Anagen Phase: Keeping hair in the active "growth" stage for longer.
  • Increasing Vascularization: Improving blood flow to the scalp to deliver essential nutrients to the bulb.
  • Follicular Survival: Stimulating stem cells within the follicle to promote regeneration and repair.

Alopecia and Thinning Concerns

PRP is most effective for specific types of hair loss, including:

  • Androgenetic Alopecia: Common male and female pattern baldness.
  • Telogen Effluvium: Excessive shedding caused by stress, illness, or hormonal changes.
  • Alopecia Areata: Helping to regrow hair in patchy areas (often used alongside other therapies).
  • Post-Transplant Support: Improving the "take rate" and healing of newly transplanted hair grafts.

The Treatment Process: Step-by-Step

A PRP hair session is a quick clinical procedure taking approximately 30 to 45 minutes:

  1. Blood Collection: A small amount of blood is drawn from your arm.
  2. Centrifugation: The blood is spun in a centrifuge to separate the platelets (liquid gold) from the red blood cells.
  3. Scalp Injection: Using a very fine needle, the PRP is injected into the scalp at the level of the hair follicles in the thinning areas.
  4. Activation: Sometimes a microneedling device is used simultaneously to further stimulate the scalp’s healing response.

Expected Results and Success Rates

PRP is not an "instant fix" but a biological journey. Most patients follow this timeline:

  • Month 1-2: A significant reduction in hair shedding is typically noticed.
  • Month 3-6: Increased hair density and a "fuller" feel as the hair shafts thicken.
  • Protocol: For optimal results, an initial course of 3 to 4 sessions (spaced 4 weeks apart) is highly recommended.

Safety and Recovery

Because the treatment uses your own blood, there is zero risk of allergic reaction or rejection. Recovery is immediate:

  • Downtime: You can return to work and your daily routine immediately after the session.
  • Aftercare: We recommend waiting 24 hours before washing your hair or using harsh hair products.
  • Sensation: You may feel mild tenderness or a "tight" sensation in the scalp for a few hours.

Who is an Ideal Candidate?

The best candidates for PRP are those in the early stages of thinning. It is much easier to save a struggling follicle than to revive one that has been dormant for years. You are an ideal candidate if:

  • You have noticed a widening part or a receding hairline.
  • Your hair feels less "dense" or your ponytail feels thinner.
  • You want a natural alternative or supplement to medications like Minoxidil or Finasteride.
  • You are not fully bald (as PRP requires some active follicles to work).
