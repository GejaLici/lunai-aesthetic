What is Jawline Contouring?

Jawline Contouring is a collection of aesthetic procedures designed to enhance the mandibular (jaw) area. The goal is to create a clear separation between the face and the neck, providing a more youthful, "snatched" appearance. Whether you seek to soften a square jaw or add definition to a "weak" jawline, contouring provides structural balance to the entire face.

Surgical vs. Non-Surgical Methods

We tailor the approach based on whether the concern is bone structure, muscle bulk, or excess fat:

  • Jaw Implants: Custom-shaped implants added to the angles of the jaw to create width and a stronger "L-shaped" profile.
  • Masseter Botox: A non-surgical option to slim the face by relaxing the chewing muscles, ideal for a "square" face shape.
  • Dermal Fillers: Temporary injections that provide immediate definition to the jawline with zero downtime.
  • Bone Contouring: Surgical reshaping of the bone for permanent results.

V-Line Surgery & Jaw Shaving

For patients desiring a slimmer, more feminine facial shape, we offer V-Line Contouring. This advanced procedure involves:

  • Mandible Shaving: Reducing the width of the jawbone for a smoother curve.
  • Chin Refinement: Often combined with a genioplasty to narrow the chin, creating a cohesive "V" shape.
  • Angle Reduction: Smoothing out prominent jaw angles for a softer, more elegant transition.

The Role of Neck Liposuction

Sometimes, a sharp jawline is simply hidden beneath localized fat. Submental Liposuction (neck lipo) is frequently performed alongside jawline contouring to:

  • Remove "double chin" fat deposits.
  • Expose the natural bone structure of the mandible.
  • Tighten the skin under the chin for a more acute neck angle.

The Procedure Step-by-Step

Surgical jawline contouring is typically performed under general anesthesia and takes 2 to 3 hours:

  1. Incision: Most incisions are made inside the mouth to ensure no visible scarring on the face.
  2. Sculpting: Specialized medical saws or burrs are used to reshape the bone, or implants are placed in sub-periosteal pockets.
  3. Closure: Internal dissolvable sutures are used, followed by a compression wrap around the jaw.

Recovery and Results

Recovery depends on the extent of the bone work performed:

  • Weeks 1-2: Significant swelling is normal. A soft-food or liquid diet may be required if incisions were intraoral.
  • Month 1: Most swelling subsides, and the new jawline begins to emerge.
  • Final Results: The "refined" look is usually fully visible at 3 to 6 months as the soft tissues settle over the new bone shape.

Who is an Ideal Candidate?

You may be a candidate for jawline contouring if:

  • You feel your jawline is too wide, too narrow, or lacks definition.
  • You have reached full bone maturity (usually 20+ years old).
  • You are in good health and have realistic goals for facial symmetry.
  • You are bothered by the appearance of a "weak" chin or jowls.
