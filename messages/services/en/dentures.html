What are Dentures?

Dentures are removable dental appliances designed to replace missing teeth and the surrounding soft tissues. Unlike the "false teeth" of the past, modern dentures are made from advanced resins and porcelains that mimic the translucency of natural enamel and the healthy hue of gum tissue. They are an effective, affordable solution for patients who have lost teeth due to decay, injury, or periodontal disease.

Types of Dentures: Full vs. Partial

We provide various options based on how many teeth need replacement:

  • Complete (Full) Dentures: Used when all teeth are missing in either the upper or lower arch. They sit on the gums and are held in place by suction or a thin layer of adhesive.
  • Partial Dentures: Ideal if you still have some healthy natural teeth. A partial denture consists of replacement teeth attached to a gum-colored base, often secured by metal or "invisible" clips that anchor to your existing teeth.
  • Immediate Dentures: Placed on the same day your teeth are extracted, ensuring you never have to spend a day without a smile while your gums heal.

Implant-Supported Dentures (Overdentures)

For patients who struggle with "slipping" or loose dentures, Implant-Supported Dentures offer the ultimate stability. By placing 2 to 4 dental implants in the jawbone, the denture can "snap" into place. This prevents movement while eating or speaking and helps preserve the jawbone from shrinking over time.

The Custom Fitting Process

Creating a denture that looks natural and feels comfortable requires precision:

  1. Impressions: We take detailed molds and digital scans of your jaws to ensure an anatomical fit.
  2. Bite Alignment: We measure how your upper and lower jaws relate to each other to ensure a comfortable bite.
  3. Wax Try-in: You will try on a wax model of your future denture to approve the color, shape, and overall look before the final version is crafted.
  4. Final Fitting: The final denture is placed, and precise adjustments are made to eliminate any "sore spots."

Life with Dentures: Adjustment & Care

It takes a few weeks to get used to the feel of new dentures. To ensure they last 5 to 10 years, follow these care steps:

  • Daily Cleaning: Use a soft-bristle brush and non-abrasive denture cleaner to remove food and plaque.
  • Overnight Soaking: Dentures must stay moist to keep their shape. Soak them in water or a mild denture solution overnight.
  • Gum Health: Even with dentures, it is vital to brush your gums, tongue, and palate every morning to stimulate circulation and remove bacteria.

Aesthetic and Health Benefits

Dentures provide more than just a new smile:

  • Facial Support: They prevent the facial muscles from sagging, which can make a person look older.
  • Improved Nutrition: Allows you to eat a wider variety of healthy, fibrous foods.
  • Speech: Helps in pronouncing words clearly that may have been difficult with missing teeth.

Who is a Candidate for Dentures?

You may be a candidate for dentures if:

  • You are missing multiple teeth or an entire arch of teeth.
  • Your remaining teeth are compromised by advanced decay or gum disease.
  • You are looking for a cost-effective alternative to full-mouth dental implants.
  • You have sufficient gum tissue to support a removable appliance.
