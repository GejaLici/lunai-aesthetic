What is PRP Face Treatment?

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) is a concentrated source of autologous platelets found in your own blood. These platelets contain powerful proteins and growth factors that are essential for tissue repair and regeneration. When applied to the face, PRP acts as a biological "booster," stimulating the skin to repair itself, produce new collagen, and restore a healthy, vibrant glow.

The Science: Growth Factors & Healing

When the skin is aged or damaged, cellular turnover slows down. PRP therapy reintroduces high concentrations of growth factors into the dermis, which triggers:

  • Collagen Production: Firming the skin and reducing fine lines.
  • Angiogenesis: Forming new blood vessels to improve skin color and oxygenation.
  • Cellular Turnover: Rapidly replacing old, dull skin cells with fresh, healthy ones.

PRP with Microneedling (The Vampire Facial)

Popularly known as the Vampire Facial, this treatment combines microneedling with a topical application of PRP. The microneedles create "micro-channels" that allow the PRP growth factors to penetrate deep into the skin layers, significantly enhancing the smoothing effect on acne scars, large pores, and uneven texture.

PRP Injections for Volume and Dark Circles

Unlike the topical approach, PRP Injections (sometimes called the "Vampire Facelift") involve injecting the plasma directly into specific areas of concern:

  • Under-Eye Area: Thickens the delicate skin to hide dark circles and hollows.
  • Nasolabial Folds: Provides a subtle, natural volume boost to smile lines.
  • Jawline & Cheeks: Improves overall skin "snap" and elasticity.

The 3-Step Process: From Blood to Beauty

The entire procedure takes about 45 to 60 minutes and is performed in a clinical setting:

  1. The Draw: A small amount of blood (similar to a routine blood test) is taken from your arm.
  2. The Centrifuge: The blood is placed in a specialized machine that spins it at high speeds to separate the nutrient-rich platelets from the red blood cells.
  3. The Application: The resulting "liquid gold" (PRP) is then injected or microneedled back into your facial skin.

Expected Results and Recovery

Because PRP uses your own biology, the results are gradual and natural-looking:

  • Downtime: Expect 24–48 hours of mild redness or swelling, similar to a light sunburn.
  • Timeline: An immediate "glow" is visible within a week, but the full collagen-building effect peaks at 3 to 4 months.
  • Maintenance: For best results, we recommend an initial series of 3 treatments, followed by a maintenance session once or twice a year.
