What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is a revolutionary orthodontic system that uses a series of custom-made, clear plastic trays (aligners) to gradually shift your teeth into their ideal positions. Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign aligners contain no metal and are removable, making them the preferred choice for adults and teenagers who want a more discreet way to improve their smile.

Why Choose Clear Aligners?

Invisalign offers several distinct advantages over traditional metal braces:

  • Virtually Invisible: Most people won't even notice you’re wearing them.
  • Removable: You can take them out to eat, drink, brush, and floss as usual.
  • Comfort: Smooth plastic edges prevent the irritation and "poking" common with wires.
  • Predictable Results: Advanced 3D imaging allows you to see your final smile before you even start.

SmartTrack® Technology: How It Works

Invisalign aligners are made from a proprietary material called SmartTrack®. This medical-grade plastic is engineered to apply constant, gentle force to the teeth. Every 1 to 2 weeks, you switch to a new set of aligners, each designed to move specific teeth in a specific order until your bite is perfectly aligned.

The Invisalign Journey Step-by-Step

  1. The Consultation: We take a digital 3D scan of your teeth (no messy putty impressions) to map out your custom plan.
  2. Custom Fabrication: Your aligners are manufactured using high-precision 3D printing technology.
  3. The Fit: You'll receive your first sets of aligners. We may apply small, tooth-colored "attachments" to help the aligners grip your teeth.
  4. Progress Checks: You’ll visit us every 6 to 8 weeks to ensure your teeth are moving according to plan.

Life with Invisalign: Eating and Hygiene

Because the aligners are removable, they fit seamlessly into your existing lifestyle:

  • No Diet Restrictions: Enjoy popcorn, apples, and candy—just remove the trays first.
  • Easy Cleaning: Simply brush your teeth and rinse your aligners before putting them back in.
  • Compliance: To achieve the best results, you must wear your aligners for 20 to 22 hours per day.

How Long Does Treatment Take?

While every case is unique, the average Invisalign treatment lasts between 12 to 18 months. Minor corrections can sometimes be completed in as little as 6 months. We provide an estimated timeline during your initial digital scan.

Are You a Candidate for Invisalign?

Invisalign is highly effective for correcting a wide range of issues, including:

  • Crowded Teeth: When there isn't enough room in the jaw for teeth to fit naturally.
  • Gaps (Diastema): Closing unwanted spaces between teeth.
  • Overbite & Underbite: Correcting how the top and bottom teeth overlap.
  • Crossbite: When upper teeth sit inside lower teeth.
