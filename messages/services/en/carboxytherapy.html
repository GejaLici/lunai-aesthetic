What is Carboxytherapy?

Carboxytherapy is a non-surgical aesthetic treatment that involves the controlled injection of medical-grade carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) into the skin or subcutaneous tissue. Originally discovered in the Royal Spas of France in the 1930s, this "miracle gas" is now used globally to treat a wide range of concerns, from dark under-eye circles to stubborn cellulite and skin laxity.

The Bohr Effect: How It Works

The science behind carboxytherapy relies on a physiological phenomenon known as the Bohr Effect. When $CO_2$ is injected, the body perceives a localized lack of oxygen:

  • Vasodilation: Blood vessels in the area dilate, increasing blood flow.
  • Oxygen Surge: Red blood cells rush to the area to "pick up" the $CO_2$ and release a surge of fresh oxygen.
  • Neovascularization: The process stimulates the growth of new capillaries and promotes collagen synthesis.

Facial Benefits: Eyes and Skin Tightening

Carboxytherapy is one of the few treatments safe enough to use on the delicate eyelid area:

  • Dark Circles: By improving vascular circulation, it clears the "blue" congestion under the eyes.
  • Eyelid Lifting: Tightens the skin of the upper and lower eyelids for a more "awake" appearance.
  • Fine Lines: Boosts collagen to smooth out crow's feet and nasolabial folds.

Body Benefits: Cellulite and Stretch Marks

When injected deeper into the adipose (fat) tissue, carboxytherapy becomes a powerful body-contouring tool:

  • Cellulite Reduction: It breaks down fat cells and strengthens the connective tissue, smoothing out the "orange peel" texture.
  • Stretch Marks: By stimulating fibroblasts to produce new collagen, it helps fade and tighten the appearance of striae.
  • Localized Fat: Helps eliminate small pockets of fat resistant to diet, such as the chin or "love handles."

What to Expect During Treatment

A carboxytherapy session is quick, usually taking 15 to 30 minutes:

  1. The Device: A tank of $CO_2$ is connected to a flow-regulator machine.
  2. Injection: The gas is delivered via a tiny, microscopic needle.
  3. The Sensation: You may feel a slight "crackling" or pressure as the gas moves through the tissue, which subsides within minutes.
  4. Completion: The gas is naturally absorbed by the body and exhaled through the lungs.

Recovery and Session Frequency

There is virtually zero downtime with carboxytherapy:

  • Immediate Effects: The area may feel warm and look slightly flushed for 10–20 minutes.
  • Number of Sessions: For best results, a series of 6 to 12 sessions is usually recommended, spaced 1 week apart.
  • Maintenance: Results can be maintained with a single treatment every few months.
