What is a Facelift?

A Facelift, medically termed a Rhytidectomy, is a surgical procedure designed to improve visible signs of aging in the face and neck. As we age, facial tissues lose volume and skin loses elasticity, leading to jowls, a sagging jawline, and deep creases around the mouth. A modern facelift repositions the underlying muscles and removes excess skin to restore a firmer, more refreshed appearance.

The SMAS Advantage: More Than Skin Deep

Older facelift methods often relied on pulling the skin tight, which could lead to a "windblown" look. Our clinic specializes in the SMAS (Superficial Musculoaponeurotic System) Lift:

  • Structural Support: Instead of just pulling skin, we lift the deeper muscular layer of the face.
  • Natural Results: Tension is placed on the muscles, not the skin, allowing for a soft, natural expression.
  • Longevity: Lifting the deeper structures provides a foundation that lasts significantly longer than skin-only lifts.

Types of Facelift Procedures

Facial aging happens differently for everyone. We tailor our approach to your needs:

  • Full Facelift: Addresses the lower two-thirds of the face and the neck.
  • Mini Facelift: Ideal for younger patients with mild jowling; utilizes shorter incisions and focuses on the mid-face.
  • Deep Plane Facelift: An advanced technique that lifts the entire facial unit as one, offering the most comprehensive rejuvenation for severe sagging.
  • Neck Lift (Platysmaplasty): Often performed alongside a facelift to remove "turkey neck" and define the jawline.

The Procedure Step-by-Step

A facelift is typically performed under general anesthesia or deep sedation and takes 3 to 5 hours:

  1. Incision: Carefully placed along the hairline and around the ear to ensure scars are hidden in natural contours.
  2. Lifting: The SMAS layer is tightened and secured with internal sutures.
  3. Redraping: The skin is gently draped back over the new contours, and excess skin is precisely trimmed.
  4. Drainage: In some cases, small drains may be placed to prevent fluid buildup during the first 24 hours.

Recovery Timeline & Care

Patience during the healing process ensures the best aesthetic outcome:

  • Days 1-3: You will wear a compression wrap around the face. Minimal discomfort is managed with prescribed medication.
  • Week 1: Most sutures are removed. Swelling and bruising are at their peak but begin to subside.
  • Week 2: Many patients feel comfortable returning to work and social settings, often using light makeup to cover any residual bruising.
  • Month 1: Most swelling is gone, and the "tight" sensation begins to relax into a natural look.

How Long Do Results Last?

A facelift does not stop the aging process, but it does "reset" the clock:

  • Long-Term Value: Most patients enjoy their results for 10 to 15 years.
  • Maintenance: Using medical-grade skincare and sun protection will help preserve your surgical results.

Are You an Ideal Candidate?

You may be a candidate for a Rhytidectomy if you have:

  • Loss of skin elasticity and muscle tone in the face and neck.
  • Deep "marionette lines" or nasolabial folds.
  • A jawline that has become obscured by jowls.
  • Good bone structure and overall physical health.
