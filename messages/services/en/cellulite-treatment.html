What is Cellulite?

Cellulite is a condition where the skin appears to have a dimpled, "orange peel," or "cottage cheese" texture. It occurs when fat deposits push through the connective tissue (septa) beneath the skin. While it is not a medical concern, many patients seek treatment to improve skin texture on the thighs, buttocks, and abdomen, where cellulite is most prominent.

The Science: Why Dimples Form

Cellulite is a structural issue, not just a "fat" issue. Three factors contribute to its appearance:

  • Fibrous Bands: The septa (connective bands) pull the skin down.
  • Fat Pockets: Fat cells push up against the skin.
  • Thinning Skin: As we age, skin loses elasticity, making the underlying structure more visible.

Subcision: Releasing the Tension

For deep, visible "craters" or dimples, Subcision is the most effective surgical solution. Using a specialized needle or the Cellfina™ system, the surgeon:

  • Precisely targets the tight fibrous bands.
  • Gently "snaps" or releases the bands responsible for pulling the skin down.
  • Allows the skin to bounce back and smooth out instantly.

Radiofrequency & Shockwave Therapy

For generalized skin waviness and laxity, non-invasive energy devices provide excellent results:

  • Radiofrequency (RF): Heats the deeper layers of the skin to stimulate collagen and thicken the dermis.
  • Acoustic Wave Therapy (AWT): Uses pressure waves to break up fat and improve lymphatic drainage, reducing the "congested" look of cellulite.
  • Vacuum-Assisted Massage: Improves circulation and helps redistribute fat cells.

What to Expect During Treatment

Procedures vary by intensity but generally follow these steps:

  1. Mapping: The provider identifies and circles specific dimples while the patient is standing.
  2. Treatment: For subcision, local anesthesia is used. For RF or Shockwave, a cooling gel is applied.
  3. Duration: Most sessions take 30 to 60 minutes.

Recovery and Longevity of Results

Patience and consistency are key to cellulite management:

  • Downtime: Non-invasive treatments have zero downtime. Subcision may cause minor bruising for 1–2 weeks.
  • Results: Subcision can provide results lasting 2 to 3 years. Energy treatments usually require a series of 6–8 sessions for optimal smoothing.
  • Expectations: While "cures" for cellulite do not exist, modern treatments can provide a 50–75% improvement in appearance.

Ideal Candidates

You may be a candidate for professional cellulite treatment if:

  • You have visible dimpling that bothers you.
  • You are at a stable weight (weight loss can sometimes make cellulite more visible due to skin laxity).
  • You have good skin elasticity.
  • You understand that maintenance treatments may be necessary to keep skin smooth.
