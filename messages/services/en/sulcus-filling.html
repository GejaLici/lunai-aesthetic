What is Sulcus Filling?

Sulcus Filling is a cosmetic injectable treatment designed to soften the nasolabial sulcus—the deep indentations or "smile lines" that run from the corners of the nose down to the corners of the mouth. As we age, the loss of facial fat and the descent of the cheek tissues make these grooves more prominent. Filling this sulcus restores a smoother transition between the cheek and the lip, creating a refreshed and less "tired" look.

Understanding the Nasolabial Sulcus

The "sulcus" is a natural anatomical feature, but several factors can cause it to deepen over time:

  • Fat Loss: The depletion of the malar (cheek) fat pads.
  • Bone Resorption: Changes in the underlying facial skeleton that provide less support for the skin.
  • Repetitive Expression: Years of smiling and talking deepen the crease in the skin.
  • Collagen Depletion: Reduced skin elasticity allows the "heavier" cheek tissue to fold over the sulcus.

Hyaluronic Acid vs. Biostimulators

We choose the filling material based on the depth of the fold and your skin quality:

  • Hyaluronic Acid (HA) Fillers: (e.g., Juvéderm, Restylane) The most popular choice. These provide immediate volume, are reversible, and integrate naturally into the tissue.
  • Biostimulators: (e.g., Sculptra, Radiesse) These work by stimulating your body's own collagen production. They are ideal for patients seeking a more gradual, structural improvement over several months.

The Treatment Experience

Sulcus filling is a "lunchtime" procedure that typically takes 15 to 30 minutes:

  1. Assessment: We analyze your facial proportions while smiling and at rest.
  2. Numbing: A topical numbing cream or a filler containing lidocaine is used to ensure comfort.
  3. Injection: Using either a fine needle or a blunt-tipped cannula, the filler is placed strategically into the deep sulcus.
  4. Refining: The area is gently massaged to ensure the product is smooth and symmetrical.

The Importance of Cheek Support

Modern aesthetics focuses on the cause rather than just the symptom. Often, a deep sulcus is caused by sagging cheeks. In many cases, we recommend a small amount of filler in the mid-face (cheekbones) to "lift" the skin away from the mouth before directly filling the sulcus. This provides a more natural, "non-puffy" result.

Results and Longevity

You will see an immediate softening of the lines after your session:

  • Downtime: Expect minimal swelling or redness for 24–48 hours. Occasional bruising may occur.
  • Longevity: Depending on the product used and your metabolism, results typically last 6 to 12 months for HA fillers and up to 2 years for biostimulators.

Who is an Ideal Candidate?

You are an ideal candidate for sulcus filling if:

  • You have deep creases or "shadows" around the nose and mouth.
  • You feel your smile lines make you look older or sadder than you feel.
  • You have realistic expectations and want a subtle, rejuvenated appearance.
  • Note: This treatment is not recommended for those with active skin infections or certain autoimmune conditions.
