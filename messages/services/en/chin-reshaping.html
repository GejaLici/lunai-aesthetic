What is Chin Reshaping?

Chin Reshaping, also known as Mentoplasty or Genioplasty, is a surgical procedure designed to improve the size, shape, and position of the chin. Because the chin acts as a foundational anchor for the face, even a subtle adjustment can dramatically improve the appearance of the neck, jawline, and nose, creating a more balanced and attractive profile.

Chin Implants vs. Sliding Genioplasty

Depending on your bone structure and goals, we utilize two primary methods:

  • Chin Implants (Augmentation): A biocompatible silicone implant is placed over the existing jawbone. This is ideal for adding projection to a "weak" or receding chin with minimal downtime.
  • Sliding Genioplasty: The surgeon carefully cuts the chin bone and moves it forward, backward, or vertically. This is a permanent solution that can also correct functional issues and severe asymmetry.

Correcting Weak Chins and Asymmetry

Chin reshaping is often the "missing piece" in facial rejuvenation. It can address several common concerns:

  • Microgenia (Receding Chin): Strengthening a chin that disappears into the neck.
  • The "Large Nose" Illusion: A small chin often makes the nose appear larger than it is; reshaping the chin restores balance.
  • Jawline Definition: Creating a sharp, masculine or feminine jawline contour.
  • Double Chin Reduction: By increasing chin projection, the skin under the neck is pulled tighter, reducing the appearance of a double chin.

The Procedure Step-by-Step

Chin surgery is usually performed under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation, taking approximately 60 to 90 minutes:

  1. Incision Placement: Incisions are typically made inside the mouth (intraoral) or in the natural crease under the chin, leaving no visible scarring.
  2. Reshaping: For implants, a pocket is created; for genioplasty, the bone is reshaped using precision medical saws.
  3. Securing: Implants are sutured in place, while bone shifts in a genioplasty are secured with small titanium plates and screws.
  4. Closure: The incision is closed with dissolvable stitches.

Recovery and Aftercare

Patients are often surprised by the manageable nature of chin surgery recovery:

  • Days 1-5: Some swelling and a "tight" sensation are normal. You will likely be on a soft-food diet if the incision was made inside the mouth.
  • Week 1: Most patients return to work. External tape or bandages are removed.
  • Activity: Avoid strenuous impact sports for 6 weeks to ensure the bone or implant remains perfectly positioned.

Safety and Long-term Results

Chin reshaping offers some of the highest satisfaction rates in plastic surgery:

  • Permanence: Both implants and genioplasty provide lifelong results.
  • Nerve Safety: Our surgeons use advanced mapping to avoid the mental nerve, which provides sensation to the lower lip.
  • Natural Feel: Once healed, the chin feels like your own bone structure.

Who is an Ideal Candidate?

You may be a candidate for chin reshaping if:

  • You have a receding or "weak" chin that affects your profile.
  • You have reached physical maturity (the jawbone has finished growing).
  • You have realistic expectations about facial symmetry.
  • You are looking to enhance the results of a Rhinoplasty or Facelift.
