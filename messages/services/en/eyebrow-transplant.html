What is an Eyebrow Transplant?

An Eyebrow Transplant is a minimally invasive hair restoration procedure that moves healthy hair follicles—typically from the back of the scalp—to the brow area. Unlike microblading or tattooing, which use pigment, a transplant provides real, growing hair that can be trimmed and shaped, offering a permanent solution to thin or non-existent brows.

Common Causes of Eyebrow Thinning

Many patients seek eyebrow restoration due to a variety of factors, including:

  • Over-plucking: Long-term hair removal can lead to permanent follicle damage.
  • Genetics: Naturally thin or light-colored brow hair.
  • Medical Conditions: Hypothyroidism, alopecia, or hormonal imbalances.
  • Trauma: Scars from accidents, burns, or previous facial surgeries.

The Technology: FUE vs. DHI

We use microsurgical techniques to ensure the most natural result with no visible scarring:

  • FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction): Individual follicles are harvested and placed into tiny, angled incisions in the brow area.
  • DHI (Direct Hair Implantation): Using a "Choi Implanter Pen," follicles are placed directly into the skin without pre-cut channels. This is often preferred for eyebrows as it allows for extreme precision in hair angle.

The Art of Brow Design

Creating a natural eyebrow is significantly more complex than a standard hair transplant. Our surgeons focus on:

  • Directional Growth: Brow hair grows at an extremely flat angle against the skin. We mirror this to prevent hair from growing outward.
  • The Arch & Tail: We customize the shape to match your facial symmetry and bone structure.
  • Single-Hair Grafts: Only single-hair follicles are used to mimic the delicate nature of real eyebrow hair.

The Procedure Step-by-Step

The surgery is performed under local anesthesia and typically takes 2 to 4 hours:

  1. Design: The surgeon draws the new brow shape for your approval.
  2. Harvesting: A small strip of hair is trimmed at the back of the head to extract donor follicles.
  3. Implantation: Between 50 to 400 grafts are placed per eyebrow, depending on the desired density.

Recovery and Growth Cycle

Healing is rapid, but the results require patience:

  • Days 1-5: Tiny crusts form around the hairs. Some pinkness or minor swelling is expected.
  • Weeks 2-3: "Shock loss" occurs where the transplanted hairs fall out—this is a normal part of the follicle's resting phase.
  • Months 4-6: New hair begins to grow. Because it is scalp hair, it will grow faster than original brow hair and requires regular trimming.
  • 12 Months: The final, full result is achieved.

Ideal Candidates

You may be a candidate for an eyebrow transplant if you:

  • Have realistic expectations and understand the need for ongoing trimming.
  • Are in good health and have no underlying skin diseases in the brow area.
  • Have sufficient donor hair on the back of your scalp.
  • Want to eliminate the daily need for brow pencils or temporary makeup.
