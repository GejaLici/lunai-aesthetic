What is Rhinoplasty?

Rhinoplasty, commonly known as a "nose job," is a specialized surgical procedure designed to modify the shape, size, and structure of the nose. Whether for cosmetic enhancement or functional improvement, this surgery reshapes the bone and cartilage to create a profile that harmonizes perfectly with your facial features.

Types of Rhinoplasty Surgery

Every nose is unique, and our surgical approach is tailored to your specific anatomy:

  • Aesthetic Rhinoplasty: Focuses on the "bridge," tip, or nostril width to improve visual balance.
  • Septorhinoplasty: Combines cosmetic reshaping with the correction of a deviated septum to resolve breathing difficulties.
  • Closed Rhinoplasty: Performed through internal incisions, leaving no visible external scars.
  • Revision Rhinoplasty: A corrective procedure for patients who have had previous nasal surgery elsewhere.

The Procedure Step-by-Step

Our clinic prioritizes patient safety and comfort. Here is what you can expect during the intervention:

  1. Pre-Operative Screening: Comprehensive blood tests, ECG, and consultations with our cardiologist and anesthesiologist.
  2. Anesthesia: We utilize advanced Total Intravenous General Anesthesia to minimize post-operative nausea and ensure a stable recovery.
  3. Surgical Sculpting: The surgeon carefully adjusts the bone and cartilage. The procedure typically lasts between 60 to 120 minutes.
  4. Initial Recovery: Patients usually spend one night in the clinic for monitoring. We use modern, breathable tampons that allow you to breathe through your nose immediately after surgery.

Recovery Timeline & Tips

A smooth recovery is essential for the best final result. Follow these guidelines:

  • Days 1–5: Keep your head elevated. Nasal tampons are typically removed during this window.
  • Days 7–10: The external plastic support (splint) is removed. Most bruising around the eyes begins to fade.
  • The First Month: Avoid strenuous exercise, direct sun exposure, and wearing heavy glasses.
  • Final Result: While the primary swelling subsides quickly, the nose continues to refine. Final results are usually seen between 6 to 10 months post-op.

Potential Risks and Considerations

As with any surgical intervention, it is important to be aware of potential risks, including:

  • Minor infections or hematomas (managed with antibiotics/care).
  • Temporary numbness in the nasal skin.
  • Aesthetic variations (why realistic expectations are vital).
  • Sensitivity to certain medical materials.

Ideal Candidates & Age Requirements

Timing is key for nasal surgery. We recommend waiting until the nasal structure is fully developed:

  • Females: Generally from age 16.
  • Males: Generally after late adolescence (17-18).
  • Older Adults: We perform a detailed evaluation of skin elasticity and thickness, as these factors influence the definition of the final result.
