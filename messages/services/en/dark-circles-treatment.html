What Causes Dark Circles?

Effective treatment begins with identifying the root cause of your dark circles. They are rarely caused by lack of sleep alone. The three primary types include:

  • Hollow Shadows (Structural): Caused by a deep "tear trough" or volume loss that creates a shadow under the eye.
  • Hyperpigmentation (Pigmentary): Excess melanin production, often due to sun damage, genetics, or friction.
  • Vascular Congestion (Vascular): Thin skin makes the underlying blue/purple blood vessels more visible.

Tear Trough Fillers for Hollowing

If your dark circles are caused by shadows from a sunken under-eye area, Dermal Fillers are the gold standard. By injecting a soft Hyaluronic Acid (HA) gel into the tear trough, we can:

  • Smooth the transition between the lower eyelid and the cheek.
  • Eliminate the "valley" that creates dark shadows.
  • Provide instant hydration and a subtle lift to the mid-face.

Laser Treatments for Pigmentation

For dark circles caused by true skin discoloration or visible veins, advanced laser technology offers a precise solution:

  • Q-Switched or Pico Lasers: Break up brown pigment (melanin) so the body can naturally clear it.
  • Vascular Lasers (IPL or Long-pulsed Nd:YAG): Target the bluish veins and redness responsible for "vascular" dark circles.
  • Fractional CO2: Stimulates collagen to thicken the delicate under-eye skin, making underlying vessels less visible.

Chemical Peels & Mesotherapy

In-office skin treatments can significantly improve the texture and tone of the periorbital area:

  • Specialized Under-Eye Peels: Low-concentration acids (like Glycolic or TCA) gently exfoliate the skin to lighten dark pigment.
  • Mesotherapy: Micro-injections of vitamins, amino acids, and lightening agents (like Vitamin C or Tranexamic Acid) directly into the dermis.
  • PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma): Uses your own growth factors to regenerate tissue and improve skin quality naturally.

What to Expect During Treatment

Most dark circle treatments are "lunchtime" procedures performed in under 30 minutes:

  1. Skin Analysis: We use specialized lighting to determine if your circles are pigmentary or structural.
  2. Preparation: The area is cleansed, and a topical numbing cream may be applied for maximum comfort.
  3. Application: Whether filler or laser, the treatment is administered with high precision to protect the eye.
  4. Aftercare: A cooling gel or recovery serum is applied to minimize immediate redness.

Recovery and Maintenance

Results and downtime vary by the method used:

  • Downtime: For fillers, you may have minor swelling for 2-3 days. For lasers, slight redness or "bronzing" of the skin may last a week.
  • Longevity: Fillers typically last 9–12 months. Laser results can be permanent with proper sun protection.
  • Home Care: We recommend medical-grade eye creams containing Caffeine, Retinol, or Vitamin K to support your results.

Who is a Good Candidate?

Under-eye rejuvenation is suitable for almost anyone bothered by a "permanently tired" look. You are an ideal candidate if:

  • You have noticeable hollowing or "bags" under the eyes.
  • Concealer is no longer enough to hide the discoloration.
  • You have realistic expectations and are looking for a non-surgical solution.
  • You do not have active skin infections or severe eye conditions.
