Arm Lift (Brachioplasty)

What is an Arm Lift? Achieving Contoured Upper Arms

An arm lift, or brachioplasty, is a surgical procedure to reshape the upper arms by removing excess skin and fat. It is commonly performed after significant weight loss or due to aging to correct the appearance of drooping, sagging arms, often referred to as "bat wings."

The Brachioplasty Procedure

During the procedure, an incision is made on the inside or back of the arm, and excess skin and fat are removed. The remaining skin is then tightened and redraped to create a smoother, more toned arm profile. Liposuction may be used in conjunction for better contouring.

Benefits and Recovery

The primary benefit is a more youthful and proportionate appearance of the arms. Recovery involves wearing compression garments and limiting strenuous activity for several weeks. Scars will fade over time, and results are long-lasting with a stable weight.
