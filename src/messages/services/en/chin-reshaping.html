What is Chin Reshaping? Achieving Facial Balance

Chin reshaping surgery, known as genioplasty or mentoplasty, is a procedure designed to improve facial proportions by altering the size and shape of the chin. A well-defined chin is a cornerstone of an attractive profile. This surgery can correct a recessed chin (microgenia), a protruding chin, or an asymmetric chin, bringing it into harmony with your other facial features.

The Genioplasty Procedure

Our surgeons offer two primary techniques for chin augmentation: the use of a custom-fit chin implant or a sliding genioplasty, which involves surgically moving the chin bone itself. Chin reduction is also possible by carefully sculpting the bone. The procedure is tailored to your unique anatomy and aesthetic goals.

Benefits and Recovery

Chin reshaping can have a powerful effect on overall facial balance, strengthening the jawline and making a prominent nose appear smaller. The surgery is often performed through a small incision inside the mouth or discreetly under the chin. Recovery typically takes about one to two weeks, with the result being a permanent improvement in your facial profile.
